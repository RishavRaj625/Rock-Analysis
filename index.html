Rock Details:

Igneous Rock

Igneous rocks are “fire-born,” meaning that they are formed from the cooling and solidification of molten (melted) rock. The word igneous derives from ignis, the Latin word for “fire.” Molten rock material is known as magma until it is erupted onto the surface when it then is termed lava.

Sedimentary Rocks

Sedimentary rocks are formed when organic or mineral particles accumulate on the Earth's surface and are compacted and cemented together. The process of particles settling in place is called sedimentation. Common sedimentary rocks include sandstone, limestone, and shale. Start as sediments carried in rivers

Metamorphic Rocks

Metamorphic rocks are formed when existing rocks are transformed by intense heat, pressure, and hot fluids deep within the Earth or where tectonic plates meet. The process of metamorphism doesn't melt the rocks, but instead changes them into denser, more compact rocks with new minerals and textures
