Interactive Geological Learning Module

1. Crystallography Systems

Learn about symmetry axes and geometry grouping:

• Cubic: 3 equal axes at 90° (Pyrite)
• Hexagonal: 4 axes, 3 at 120° in plane, 1 vertical (Quartz)
• Monoclinic: 3 unequal axes, 1 oblique angle (Gypsum, Talc, Mica, Feldspar)
• Trigonal: Rhombohedral shapes (Calcite, Hematite)

2. Mohs Hardness Testing

Determine scratch resistance compared to common items:

• Hardness 1-2: Scratched by fingernail (Talc, Gypsum)
• Hardness 3: Scratched by copper coin (Calcite)
• Hardness 4-5: Scratched by pocket knife/glass (Apatite)
• Hardness 6-7: Scratches glass; steel file (Feldspar, Quartz)
